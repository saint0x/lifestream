# Lifestream Lattice

## Remaining Media-Plane Scope

- RTMP/SRT/WebRTC ingest edge with authenticated session binding into the control plane
- FFmpeg-driven transcode ladder orchestration for live ABR, VOD mezzanine normalization, and thumbnail or preview extraction
- Segment packaging for HLS and low-latency live playback, including DVR windows, archive rollover, and signed playback manifests
- Recording, clip extraction, and collaborative split-output rendering for host, guest mirror, and shared-program archives
- Storage lifecycle management across hot media, archives, derivatives, and deletion or takedown workflows
- Player-facing playback runtime contracts for live, VOD, episodic, and collaborative layouts
- Media telemetry, QoS signals, and operator controls that feed back into the control plane without breaking source-of-truth ownership
