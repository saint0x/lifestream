# Product Surfaces

This folder explains Vanta's internal and adjacent product surfaces for new onboardees.

The important product map is simple:

- [Creator Ad Hub](./creator-ad-hub.md) is the creator-side advertising workspace inside the main Vanta frontend at `frontend/src/pages/AdHubPage.tsx`.
- [External Ad Hub](./external-ad-hub.md) is the separate external media-buyer dashboard in `external-ad-hub/` for discovering and buying Vanta inventory.
- [Vanta Video Editor](./vanta-video-editor.md) is the free episodic-series editing workspace for creators building premium Vanta programming.
- [Vanta OBS Editor](./vanta-obs-editor.md) is the free live-streaming and recording studio for creators on the live side of the platform.

Together, these tools support the core Vanta model:

```text
Creators make high-quality programming
-> creators promote and concentrate audience attention
-> Vanta packages that attention into sellable inventory
-> external buyers purchase through Vanta
-> creators execute and prove the work
-> Vanta captures the infrastructure spread in the middle
```

The creator tools make creators stronger. The buyer tools make demand easier to capture. The business exists in the infrastructure connecting both sides.

Do not confuse the two Ad Hubs: the creator Ad Hub is for accepting and executing offers, while the external Ad Hub is for outside buyers purchasing inventory.
