# Vanta Profile Prompts

The Vanta profile should become the creator's premium home.

Creators should publish it everywhere.

The creator benefit is clarity: one place that makes the creator's work, identity, series, links, and value legible to viewers, collaborators, and advertisers.

The profile is where creator heat should concentrate. High-quality content, aggressive self-promotion, serious self-marketing, and smart financial self-investment should all point back to a destination that makes the creator's value obvious.

The profile should also make collaboration legible. Vanta credits can show who directed, produced, edited, appeared in, executive produced, or collaborated on a project. Those links can become audience pathways between creators and projects, increasing the chance of network effects and Qualified Attention.

The profile should make it obvious what to watch, why to watch, and why to come back.

It should also make the creator easier to monetize because it gathers their premium work, identity, series, links, and audience attention in one place.

## Prompt 1: Vanta Profile Positioning

```text
Help me turn my Vanta profile into my main premium destination.

My niche:
[describe]

My audience:
[describe]

My current series:
[list]

My planned series:
[list]

My off-platform channels:
[list]

Creators and collaborators connected to my work:
[list]

Make the profile feel like my best portfolio and my premium media home, not a generic social bio. It should naturally communicate why Vanta is where my full work lives.

Create:

- profile headline;
- profile bio;
- pinned series order;
- description for each series;
- CTA for first-time visitors;
- CTA for returning viewers;
- CTA for reminders/follows;
- what link I should put in every bio;
- how I should talk about my Vanta profile on other platforms;
- how my self-promotion and paid promotion should point back to the profile;
- how collaborator credits should be presented or discussed;
- how my profile should encourage viewers to explore credited collaborators and related projects;
- how to make my profile feel like a network, not a dumping ground.

Make the profile communicate:

1. This is where my best long-form work lives.
2. New episodes are coming consistently.
3. Viewers should follow and return.
```

## Prompt 2: Profile Conversion Audit

```text
Audit my Vanta profile for viewer conversion.

Profile text:
[paste]

Series listed:
[paste]

Audience:
[describe]

Evaluate whether the profile makes Vanta feel valuable to me: a beautiful portfolio, a complete work library, an external-link hub, and the place where returning viewers build real economic value.

Evaluate whether a new viewer immediately understands:

- who I am;
- what I make;
- what to watch first;
- why the content is exclusive;
- why they should follow;
- when they should come back;
- what series are ongoing;
- who I collaborate with;
- which credits or collaborators are worth clicking into;
- what my highest-quality work is.

Give me:

- revised profile copy;
- better series descriptions;
- pinned order;
- missing CTAs;
- what feels confusing;
- what feels too small;
- what would make the profile feel more premium.
```

## Prompt 3: Link Everywhere Plan

```text
Build a plan for making my Vanta profile the main destination across the internet.

Vanta profile:
[link]

Other platforms:
[list]

Series:
[list]

Create a platform-by-platform plan for:

Every platform should point back to Vanta as my main portfolio, not just as a temporary campaign link.

- bio links;
- pinned posts;
- story highlights;
- newsletter footer;
- YouTube descriptions;
- TikTok link language;
- Instagram link language;
- X pinned post;
- community profile links;
- collaborator profile and credit links;
- reciprocal links with other creators;
- QR code or offline use if relevant;
- launch announcements;
- reminder posts.

Every channel should tell viewers:

> My best full episodes are on Vanta.

Make the language natural for each platform.
```
