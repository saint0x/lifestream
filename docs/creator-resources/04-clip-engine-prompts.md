# Clip Engine Prompts

Every strong episode should become many strong clips.

Creators should treat clips as the distribution system for the full Vanta episode.

The creator benefit is leverage: one strong episode should become many honest invitations back to the creator's best work.

Clips are how creator heat travels. In the thermal-energy model, the full episode creates substance, but clips, reposts, paid boosts, collaborations, and platform-native adaptations move that substance through the internet.

The complete episode lives on Vanta.

The best moments travel everywhere.

Vanta is the destination those clips point back to: the creator's premium profile, full episodes, series library, external links, and monetization engine.

## Prompt 1: Clip Map From An Episode

```text
Turn this Vanta episode into a clip map.

Episode title:
[title]

Episode summary or transcript:
[paste transcript, outline, or scene list]

Target platforms:
[TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X, LinkedIn, newsletter, etc.]

Find every clip-worthy moment.

Every clip should make Vanta feel like the obvious place to watch the full work, not like a random link.

For each clip, provide:

- clip title;
- timestamp or scene reference;
- hook;
- why it works;
- ideal length;
- platform fit;
- caption;
- on-screen text;
- first-frame idea;
- CTA to the full Vanta episode;
- whether it should be posted once, reposted, remixed, or turned into a thread;
- whether it deserves paid boosting or extra editing investment;
- whether it is emotional, funny, educational, controversial, beautiful, surprising, or narrative.

Then rank the clips by expected ability to bring viewers back to Vanta.

Do not only pick obvious highlights.

Find subtle scenes, reactions, behind-the-scenes moments, mistakes, transitions, reveals, and character moments that could make people care.
```

## Prompt 2: Repurpose One Scene Ten Ways

```text
Take this scene and turn it into 10 different promotional assets.

Scene:
[describe or paste transcript]

Full Vanta episode:
[describe]

Audience:
[describe]

Make each asset naturally point back to my Vanta profile as the home of the full episode and the rest of my work.

Create:

1. TikTok/Reel version.
2. YouTube Shorts version.
3. X post.
4. Instagram Story sequence.
5. Newsletter blurb.
6. Behind-the-scenes post.
7. Quote graphic copy.
8. Community discussion prompt.
9. Teaser for next episode.
10. Repost variation for one week later.

Every asset must point people back to the full Vanta episode.

Do not make the CTA generic.

Make the viewer feel there is something specific they need to watch on Vanta.
```

## Prompt 3: Clip Posting Calendar

```text
Build a 14-day clip distribution calendar for this Vanta episode.

Episode:
[describe]

Available clips:
[list clips]

Platforms:
[list platforms]

Release date:
[date]

Use Vanta as the main destination throughout the calendar. The clips should generate repeat visits to my profile, not isolated platform views.

Create a calendar that includes:

- pre-release clips;
- launch-day clips;
- post-release reminders;
- reposts;
- alternate hooks;
- behind-the-scenes posts;
- comment prompts;
- story posts;
- newsletter mentions;
- community posts;
- return-viewer reminders;
- next-episode teasers.

For each post include:

- platform;
- asset;
- copy;
- CTA;
- timing;
- why it exists.

The goal is not just views on clips.

The goal is to bring viewers to Vanta and make them return.
```

## Prompt 4: Clip Quality Critic

```text
Critique these clips before I post them.

Clip descriptions or scripts:
[paste]

Full Vanta episode:
[describe]

Judge whether each clip strengthens my Vanta profile, drives real viewers to the full episode, and attracts people likely to return.

Evaluate each clip for:

- first-second hook;
- clarity without context;
- emotional pull;
- curiosity gap;
- visual strength;
- audio strength;
- caption strength;
- CTA strength;
- likelihood to send viewers to Vanta;
- likelihood to attract returning viewers rather than one-time curiosity.

For each weak clip, rewrite:

- the opening;
- the caption;
- the on-screen text;
- the CTA;
- and the cut structure.

Be harsh. Weak clips waste distribution volume.
```
