# Advertiser Value Prompts

Creators should understand why an advertiser would care about them.

The answer is not simply follower count.

The creator becomes valuable when they consistently combine:

- high-quality content;
- a devout audience;
- trusted taste or point of view;
- clear category relevance;
- repeat viewing over time;
- strategic self-promotion;
- smart self-investment;
- and measurable Qualified Attention.

Advertisers are not only buying exposure.

They are buying access to a creator-audience relationship that can move real people.

They are also buying video advertising inventory.

That matters because digital video is one of the most valuable and fastest-moving parts of advertising. IAB reported that U.S. digital video ad spend is projected to surpass $80B in 2026, with digital video expected to exceed 60% of total TV/video ad spend for the first time. IAB also reported that U.S. creator ad spend is projected to reach $37B in 2025 and $44B in 2026.

The creator-facing lesson is not "memorize the ad market."

The lesson is:

> Vanta gives creators a place to create the kind of high-value video inventory advertisers already want to buy.

Vanta is not asking creators to be only short-form influencers. It gives creators a place to build premium 30-40 minute episodic programming: closer to an HBO, Netflix, or streaming-series environment than a disposable feed post.

That is the Vanta-specific advantage. A creator can turn their trust, taste, audience, collaborators, and consistency into a premium video property with real ad inventory around it.

The Vanta opportunity sits at the intersection of two valuable markets:

```text
creator trust and audience movement
+ premium digital video inventory
= high-value creator media property
```

A 30-40 minute Vanta episode can create:

- deeper watch time;
- richer audience intent;
- more natural sponsor surfaces;
- episode and season-level context;
- recurring release inventory;
- creator profile inventory;
- collaborator and credit-network discovery;
- clips that promote the full property;
- and measurable Qualified Attention around the creator's media property.

In IAB's 2026 digital video framing, marketers increasingly expect creator and Hollywood-style economies to work together to deliver business results. Vanta is built directly around that idea: creator trust plus premium episodic video plus measurable attention.

So the creator should understand the opportunity clearly: Vanta is not just another place to post. It is a place to create higher-value advertising inventory from work that already deserves to be treated like premium media.

The thermal-energy model still applies:

```text
high-quality content
* devout audience trust
* consistent release history
* aggressive self-promotion
* smart collaboration and self-investment
= advertiser-relevant creator heat

creator heat measured over time
= Qualified Attention

Qualified Attention
= something Vanta can help package, sell, report, renew, and turn into creator revenue
```

A creator who makes excellent work once may be interesting.

A creator who makes excellent work repeatedly, brings the same audience back, proves that audience cares, and keeps investing in the property becomes commercially valuable.

## Prompt 1: Advertiser Value Audit

```text
Audit my value to advertisers as a Vanta creator.

My niche:
[describe]

My Vanta series:
[list]

My audience:
[describe]

My current platforms:
[list]

My strongest proof of audience devotion:
[comments, watch time, repeat viewers, purchases, community activity, newsletter opens, event attendance, etc.]

My release consistency:
[describe]

My collaborators or credited contributors:
[list]

Evaluate me through the Vanta advertiser-value model.

Score me from 1-10 across:

- content quality;
- audience devotion;
- release consistency;
- repeat-viewer behavior;
- category clarity;
- advertiser fit;
- premium video inventory value;
- trust with my audience;
- ability to move viewers to Vanta;
- Qualified Attention potential;
- collaboration and credit-network value;
- self-promotion discipline;
- smart self-investment;
- brand safety and suitability;
- proof that my audience takes action.

Then tell me:

- why an advertiser would care about me;
- why an advertiser might hesitate;
- what audience proof I need to collect;
- what content quality upgrades would increase my value;
- what consistency upgrades would increase my value;
- what promotion habits would increase my value;
- what collaborations could make me more valuable;
- what categories of brands should care most;
- how my Vanta episodes create stronger ad inventory than a normal feed post;
- what I should stop doing because it weakens advertiser trust;
- what I should do for the next 30 days to become more sponsor-ready.

Be direct. Do not let me confuse popularity with advertiser value.
```

## Prompt 2: Devout Audience Proof

```text
Help me prove that my audience is devout, not just large.

My audience size:
[followers, subscribers, viewers, email list, community size]

My best engagement proof:
[paste examples]

My Vanta behavior:
[views, watch time, return viewers, follows, reminders, comments, shares if known]

My audience actions outside Vanta:
[purchases, signups, event attendance, DMs, replies, community posts, etc.]

Build an audience proof file that an advertiser would understand.

Include:

- the strongest signs of audience devotion;
- weak signals that I should not overstate;
- what screenshots or data I should save;
- what testimonials or comments matter;
- what repeat behavior matters most;
- what Vanta metrics matter most;
- what off-platform metrics matter most;
- what audience segments advertisers would care about;
- how to describe my audience without exaggerating;
- what proof I still need to gather.

Frame everything around this idea:

Advertisers care about whether my audience trusts me enough to watch, return, click, buy, recommend, or pay attention on purpose.
```

## Prompt 3: Brand Fit Map

```text
Map which advertisers should care about my Vanta work.

My niche:
[describe]

My series:
[describe]

My audience:
[describe]

Brands my audience already mentions, buys, or trusts:
[list]

Brands I would never want to work with:
[list]

Create a brand fit map.

Organize advertisers into:

1. obvious premium fits;
2. mid-market fits;
3. niche or local fits;
4. experimental fits;
5. brands to avoid.

For each advertiser category, explain:

- why the audience match is real;
- why my content environment makes sense;
- why my Vanta episode or season is valuable video inventory;
- what the brand would be buying;
- what proof I need before pitching;
- what kind of integration would feel organic;
- what kind of integration would damage trust;
- whether the fit is brand, performance, community, event, product, or education-led.

Do not recommend random brands just because they have money.

Only recommend advertisers that make sense for my audience, content, and reputation.
```

## Prompt 4: Sponsor-Ready Series Upgrade

```text
Make this Vanta series more valuable to advertisers without making it feel like an ad.

Series:
[describe]

Audience:
[describe]

Current format:
[describe]

Release cadence:
[describe]

Current promotion:
[describe]

Potential advertiser categories:
[list]

Improve the series so it becomes more sponsor-ready.

Give me upgrades for:

- content quality;
- recurring format;
- audience devotion;
- consistency;
- watchability;
- brand safety;
- category clarity;
- episode structure;
- moments where a sponsor could fit naturally;
- episode, season, and profile inventory a sponsor could buy;
- why the 30-40 minute format creates more valuable attention than a short post;
- clips that prove audience interest;
- collaborations that could expand advertiser value;
- credit strategy for contributors;
- metrics I should track;
- proof I should collect after every episode.

Important: do not turn the series into brand copy.

The advertiser value should come from stronger programming, stronger audience trust, and clearer Qualified Attention.
```

## Prompt 5: Vanta Inventory Explanation

```text
Explain why my Vanta series is valuable advertising inventory.

Series:
[describe]

Episode length:
[minutes]

Release cadence:
[describe]

Audience:
[describe]

Audience devotion proof:
[describe]

Sponsor categories:
[list]

Help me explain this in creator-friendly business language.

Cover:

- why long-form Vanta episodes create deeper attention than short feed posts;
- why 30-40 minute premium episodes can support stronger sponsor context;
- why recurring episodes or seasons are more valuable than one-off uploads;
- how Vanta gives me a place to create this higher-value style of inventory;
- why my Vanta profile adds portfolio and discovery value;
- why clips should act as marketing for the full inventory;
- how collaborator credits can cross-pollinate audiences;
- what Qualified Attention proves;
- what an advertiser is actually buying;
- why this should still protect my audience trust;
- why Vanta is the right home for this kind of creator media property.

Keep the tone clear and confident.

Do not make me sound like I am begging for a sponsor.

Make me sound like a creator who understands the value of premium video inventory and a devout audience.
```

## Prompt 6: 90-Day Advertiser Value Plan

```text
Build me a 90-day plan to become more valuable to advertisers on Vanta.

My current state:
[describe honestly]

My strongest series:
[describe]

My weakest area:
[quality, consistency, promotion, audience devotion, collaboration, analytics, brand fit, etc.]

Available time:
[hours per week]

Available budget:
[budget]

Available collaborators:
[list]

Create a 90-day operating plan that improves:

- content quality;
- release consistency;
- audience return behavior;
- Vanta profile strength;
- clip output;
- self-promotion;
- self-marketing;
- financial self-investment;
- collaboration and credit-network effects;
- Qualified Attention proof;
- premium video inventory value;
- brand category clarity;
- sponsor readiness.

Break the plan into:

- days 1-30;
- days 31-60;
- days 61-90.

For each phase include:

- what to publish;
- what to improve;
- what to promote;
- what to measure;
- what to spend money on;
- who to collaborate with;
- what proof to collect;
- what would make me more valuable to advertisers;
- how to make my Vanta inventory easier to understand and buy;
- what would make me less valuable if I keep doing it.

The goal is not to beg for sponsors.

The goal is to become obviously valuable because I consistently make strong work, bring back a devout audience, and create Qualified Attention Vanta can help monetize.
```
