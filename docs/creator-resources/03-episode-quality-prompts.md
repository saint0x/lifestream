# Episode Quality Prompts

High-quality content is the first growth strategy.

No amount of posting can permanently compensate for weak episodes.

These prompts should help the creator make work that deserves the audience's time, not just work that satisfies a platform requirement.

In the thermal-energy model, episode quality is the first multiplier. Promotion can amplify strong work, but it cannot permanently rescue weak work. The creator should invest time, effort, and money where it most increases the episode's ability to hold attention and justify aggressive promotion.

Creators should make episodes strong enough that viewers want to return and advertisers feel proud to appear around the work.

Vanta gives strong episodes a premium home, a portfolio context, and a path to advertiser value. That only matters if the work is good enough to hold attention.

## Prompt 1: Episode Quality Audit

```text
Audit this episode concept for Vanta.

Episode concept:
[describe]

Series:
[describe]

Target viewer:
[describe]

My production resources:
[describe]

Evaluate whether this episode is strong enough to make viewers come back.

Use Vanta context: this episode should strengthen my premium profile, create real Qualified Attention, and give me material that can be marketed without feeling forced or fake.

Score it from 1-10 across:

- hook strength;
- story clarity;
- emotional stakes;
- visual interest;
- host/subject charisma;
- pacing potential;
- clip potential;
- return-viewer potential;
- advertiser suitability;
- production feasibility;
- Vanta exclusivity value.

Then tell me:

- what is currently weak;
- what must be added before shooting;
- what scenes are required;
- what shots are required;
- what moments could become clips;
- what promotion angles this quality unlocks;
- where spending money would most improve the episode;
- what the first 60 seconds should do;
- what the ending should make viewers want next;
- what would make this feel premium;
- what would make this feel cheap.

Be strict. The goal is not to upload. The goal is to create programming people return to.
```

## Prompt 2: Shot And Scene Plan

```text
Create a shot and scene plan for this Vanta episode:

[episode description]

I need the episode to feel high-quality, intentional, and rewatchable.

Design this for Vanta as the premium destination: the full episode should justify sending viewers off social feeds and into my portfolio.

Give me:

- opening sequence;
- required story beats;
- must-capture scenes;
- interview questions if relevant;
- B-roll list;
- close-up list;
- environmental shots;
- transition shots;
- emotional beats;
- tension moments;
- payoff moment;
- ending hook into the next episode;
- backup shots if the main footage is weak;
- audio capture checklist;
- lighting checklist;
- what not to forget on shoot day.

Also identify the scenes most likely to become short clips.
```

## Prompt 3: Make It More Cinematic Without Wasting Money

```text
I want this episode to feel more cinematic without blindly spending money.

Episode:
[describe]

Resources:
[describe]

Prioritize upgrades that help the episode feel worthy of my Vanta portfolio and valuable enough for viewers to return.

Give me practical upgrades across:

- story;
- location;
- framing;
- lighting;
- movement;
- sound;
- pacing;
- music;
- titles;
- wardrobe/production design;
- B-roll;
- opening;
- ending.

For each upgrade, tell me:

- cost;
- effort;
- quality impact;
- risk;
- whether it helps viewer retention;
- whether it helps clip performance.

Prioritize the upgrades that most improve quality and return-viewer behavior.
```

## Prompt 4: Director Study Prompt

```text
Help me study great directors and scenes for this Vanta series.

Series:
[describe]

Tone I want:
[describe]

Audience:
[describe]

Give me a study list of films, shows, YouTube series, documentaries, interviews, trailers, scenes, and creators I should study.

Connect every lesson back to making better Vanta episodes, stronger clips, and a more valuable creator portfolio.

For each reference, explain:

- what to study;
- what technique I can borrow;
- what not to copy;
- how it could improve my show;
- how it could improve my clips;
- how it could improve audience retention.

Then give me a practical exercise I can do this week to apply the lessons.
```
