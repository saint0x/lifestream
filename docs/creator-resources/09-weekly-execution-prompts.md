# Weekly Execution Prompts

Creators need cadence.

Quality matters.

Volume matters.

Returning viewers matter.

The weekly system should keep creators making, shipping, clipping, promoting, learning, and improving.

Vanta multiplies this weekly work by giving the creator a premium profile, a full-series home, and an advertising engine that can monetize real returning attention.

The creator benefit is the weekly compounding: better episodes, stronger clips, clearer audience habits, a deeper portfolio, and more proof that the creator can move real people intentionally.

The weekly thermal-energy question is:

> Did I multiply quality by promotion by marketing by self-investment this week?

If the answer is no, the creator probably did not create enough heat for Vanta to convert later.

The weekly collaboration question is:

> Did I create or strengthen a real relationship that can improve the work and cross-pollinate audiences through Vanta credits?

Strategic collaborators are not just production help. They can become clickable audience pathways that drive more profile visits, more return behavior, more Qualified Attention, and more creator revenue if the work is good and both sides promote it consistently.

## Prompt 1: Weekly Creator War Plan

```text
Build my weekly Vanta creator war plan.

My current series:
[list]

Episodes in progress:
[list]

Clips available:
[list]

Release cadence:
[describe]

Available time this week:
[hours]

Available help:
[editors, contractors, collaborators, none]

Creators or collaborators I can work with this week:
[list]

Money I can responsibly invest this week:
[budget]

Treat Vanta as my main portfolio and monetization destination. Every week should make my profile stronger, my series slate deeper, and my returning audience more consistent.

Create a 7-day plan that includes:

- episode production;
- editing;
- clip creation;
- clip posting;
- behind-the-scenes content;
- Vanta profile promotion;
- reminder/follow CTA pushes;
- community engagement;
- collaborator outreach or co-promotion;
- audience replies;
- analytics review;
- next-series development;
- director/scene study;
- AI/tool experimentation.
- smart paid or self-funded investment.
- credit cleanup for collaborators on Vanta.

Prioritize:

1. high-quality episode output;
2. clips that bring viewers to Vanta;
3. returning-viewer behavior;
4. developing more series;
5. learning and improving taste;
6. multiplying quality through promotion, marketing, and investment;
7. building collaboration paths that can cross-pollinate audiences.

Make the plan intense but executable.
```

## Prompt 2: Daily Checklist

```text
Give me a daily checklist for growing my Vanta audience today.

Context:
[what I am working on today]

Available time:
[time]

Assets available:
[clips, episode, behind-the-scenes, profile link]

Make the checklist connect daily actions to the Vanta flywheel: better work, more legitimate distribution, more profile visits, more returning viewers, more advertiser value.

Create a checklist for:

- one quality improvement;
- one clip action;
- one Vanta profile promotion;
- one return-viewer reminder;
- one community interaction;
- one collaborator or credit-network action;
- one learning/study action;
- one future series action;
- one analytics or feedback review.

Each item should be specific enough that I can do it today.

Do not let me confuse planning with output.
```

## Prompt 3: Weekly Review

```text
Run my weekly Vanta creator review.

This week I published:
[list]

This week I promoted:
[list]

This week my audience did:
[views, watch time, comments, follows, reminders, returning viewers if known]

This week I learned:
[notes]

Evaluate the week through the Vanta model: did my work make my profile more valuable, bring real viewers to full episodes, and create reasons for them to return?

Evaluate:

- did I make high-quality content?
- did I publish enough?
- did I clip enough?
- did I promote hard enough?
- did I market the release like it mattered?
- did I invest time or money in the highest-leverage places?
- did I collaborate, co-promote, or improve my credit network?
- did I push my Vanta profile everywhere?
- did I create reasons for viewers to return?
- did I develop future series?
- did I improve my taste?
- did I use AI/tools intelligently?
- where did I think too small?

Then give me:

- what to double down on;
- what to stop doing;
- what to improve next week;
- what series idea to advance;
- what distribution experiment to run;
- what return-viewer system to strengthen.
```

## Prompt 4: No-Excuses Reset

```text
I am losing momentum on Vanta.

Reset me.

Here is what is happening:
[describe honestly]

Give me a no-excuses plan for the next 72 hours.

Use Vanta as the reset anchor: my job is to make strong work, point people to my premium profile, and rebuild returning viewer momentum.

The plan must include:

- one concrete episode action;
- one quality improvement action;
- five clip/post actions;
- one Vanta profile action;
- one reminder/return-viewer action;
- one audience/community action;
- one future series action;
- one learning action from a great director, scene, trailer, or creator;
- one thing I should stop overthinking.

Be direct.

Help me remove limiting beliefs and get back to output.

The goal is simple:

Make better content, bring viewers to Vanta, and make them come back.
```
