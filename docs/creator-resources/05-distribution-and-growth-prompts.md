# Distribution And Growth Prompts

Creators should treat themselves as both a media studio and a marketing studio.

The full premium property lives on Vanta.

Everything else becomes distribution.

The creator benefit is not "post more for Vanta." The creator benefit is to stop letting strong work vanish inside disposable feeds. Distribution should help the creator make their best work easier to find, easier to remember, easier to return to, and easier to monetize.

This is the thermal-energy model in practice:

```text
high-quality episode
* aggressive self-promotion
* serious self-marketing
* smart financial self-investment
= heat that can move an audience
```

Volume matters, but it must be legitimate volume: real posts, real communities, real clips, real viewers, real attention.

Do not buy fake views, fake clicks, fake accounts, or deceptive engagement.

That damages the creator, damages Vanta, and weakens the Qualified Attention layer.

Creators may use contractors, assistants, street teams, editors, or posting help, but the work should promote real content to real audiences.

Creators may also spend real money when it is disciplined: editors, designers, thumbnails, paid boosts, travel, research, gear, location access, or distribution help. The standard is simple: investment should increase quality, increase legitimate reach, or increase returning-viewer behavior.

Collaboration is legitimate distribution when it is real. Creators should look for other successful creators, talented collaborators, guests, directors, producers, editors, or experts whose audience overlaps naturally. If both sides promote the work and both sides are credited on Vanta, the collaboration can cross-pollinate audiences and create more Qualified Attention than either creator would generate alone.

Vanta is the multiplier on that legitimate distribution. The more real viewers a creator consistently brings to high-quality Vanta episodes, the more Vanta can help package that attention for advertiser value, and the more the creator can turn promotion into durable business value instead of temporary reach.

## Prompt 1: Internet-Wide Distribution Plan

```text
Build me an aggressive distribution plan for my Vanta series.

Series:
[describe]

Target viewer:
[describe]

Platforms I have:
[list]

Platforms I should consider:
[list or ask AI to recommend]

My weekly time:
[hours]

My budget:
[budget]

Create a plan that includes:

Make Vanta the premium destination in the plan: my profile should be the link I am proud to send people to because it shows my work, series, identity, and external links in one beautiful place.

- what I post on each platform;
- how often I post;
- what clips go where;
- what behind-the-scenes content I should make;
- what community posts I should make;
- what newsletter/email pushes I should send;
- what livestreams or live discussions I should run;
- what creators or collaborators I should partner with;
- how collaborator credits and links should be used;
- how I should use my Vanta profile link;
- how I should reuse strong clips multiple times without feeling lazy;
- what contractors or assistants could help with;
- where I should consider spending money to create more heat;
- what not to automate yet;
- what can be automated once the manual system works.

The goal is returning Vanta viewers, not vanity reach.

Make the plan high-volume and realistic.
```

## Prompt 2: Red-Carpet-Style Promotion For A Creator

```text
Design a small-scale Hollywood-style promotion campaign for my Vanta episode or series.

Project:
[describe]

Release date:
[date]

Audience:
[describe]

Translate movie/TV promotion tactics into creator-scale actions.

Frame Vanta as the premiere destination: social platforms build anticipation, but the full premium work and creator portfolio live on Vanta.

- teaser trailer;
- official trailer;
- poster/thumbnail moment;
- behind-the-scenes content;
- "get ready with me" or day-in-the-life content;
- creator interviews;
- cast/guest/subject interviews if relevant;
- collaborator-led promotion;
- co-posts with credited contributors;
- countdown posts;
- premiere/live watch idea;
- post-release discussion;
- fan questions;
- deleted scenes or extra moments;
- press-style announcement copy;
- personal story about why the episode matters;
- reminders that push viewers back to Vanta.

Give me a timeline from 14 days before release through 14 days after release.

Make it intense, but possible.

Include where I should personally invest money, time, or favors to make the release feel bigger without wasting resources.
```

## Prompt 3: Contractor / Street Team Brief

```text
Create a brief for legitimate distribution helpers for my Vanta series.

Important: I do not want fake engagement, fake clicks, bots, spam, or deceptive posting.

I want real people helping me distribute real clips to real audiences.

Series:
[describe]

Target audience:
[describe]

Assets available:
[clips, captions, thumbnails, links]

Credited collaborators and their platforms:
[list]

The helpers should understand that Vanta is the destination and portfolio, not just another link. Their work should create real viewer interest and repeat visits.

If collaborators are involved, helpers should also understand the credit graph: who is credited, what profile or project each person points to, and how promotion can help audiences move between related Vanta projects.

Create:

- role description;
- allowed platforms;
- posting rules;
- community rules;
- banned behavior;
- tone guidelines;
- link usage rules;
- daily posting targets;
- reporting requirements;
- quality checklist;
- examples of good posts;
- examples of bad posts;
- escalation rules.

The helpers' job is to increase legitimate discovery of my Vanta profile and episodes.
```

## Prompt 4: Growth Hack Brainstorm

```text
Give me 50 legitimate growth experiments for bringing viewers to my Vanta series.

Series:
[describe]

Audience:
[describe]

Constraints:
[budget, time, platforms, team]

Rules:

- no fake engagement;
- no spam;
- no misleading links;
- no tricks that damage audience trust;
- every idea must point back to Vanta;
- prioritize returning viewers over one-time clicks.

Every idea should use Vanta as the home base for my premium work and should increase real audience attention that the platform can help monetize.

Organize the ideas by:

- clips;
- collaborations;
- credit-based cross-promotion;
- reciprocal work on other creators' Vanta projects;
- communities;
- behind-the-scenes;
- live events;
- newsletters;
- comments/replies;
- paid boosts;
- contests/giveaways;
- search/SEO;
- local/offline promotion;
- creator network cross-promotion.

For each idea include:

- effort;
- cost;
- expected impact;
- risk;
- how to measure it;
- how it creates repeat viewing.
```
