# Reporting And Renewals

Reporting should help the advertiser decide what to renew and help the creator understand what made their audience valuable. The creator should leave each campaign with more proof, sharper instincts, and a stronger Vanta property.

## Reporting Principle

Post-campaign reporting is not administrative cleanup.

It is the proof engine for renewal, pricing power, creator acquisition, and advertiser trust.

Every campaign should answer:

> Did this advertiser buy real, qualified, creator-imported attention?

It should also answer the thermal-energy question:

> How much heat did the creator create, how much of it became measurable Qualified Attention, and how much money can Vanta convert from that proof next time?

## Minimum Campaign Report

Every advertiser report should include:

- campaign name;
- advertiser;
- creator or category;
- campaign dates;
- package purchased;
- placements delivered;
- creator promotion delivered;
- total views;
- qualified viewers;
- qualified-viewer rate;
- Verified Viewer Score;
- average watch minutes;
- completion or retention signals;
- returning viewer rate;
- engagement signals;
- traffic attribution;
- notable audience behavior;
- delivery versus plan;
- recommendation for renewal.

If available, include Creator Attention Value and value capture analysis.

The report should make the conversion chain visible:

```text
Creator content quality
-> creator promotion delivered
-> audience heat
-> Qualified Attention
-> advertiser value
-> renewal / expansion
```

## Qualified Attention Explanation

Reports should explain Qualified Attention in plain language.

Default language:

> Vanta does not report only raw impressions. Vanta measures Qualified Attention: viewers who meaningfully watched, returned, engaged, or otherwise demonstrated higher-quality attention around the sponsored creator property.

The goal is to make the advertiser trust that Vanta is filtering for attention quality.

Do not bury this in technical language.

## Measurement Transparency

Attention measurement is only valuable if the buyer can understand what produced it.

Every report should disclose:

- metric definitions;
- measurement window;
- algorithm version;
- qualified-viewer threshold;
- identity and attribution confidence notes;
- invalid-traffic or low-quality traffic handling;
- which signals were measured;
- which signals were not measured;
- whether any third-party verification was used.

Do not let Qualified Attention become a black box.

The score should create trust because the components are visible.

Qualified Attention should complement the rest of the report.

Do not present it as the only campaign truth.

The report should connect attention to the campaign's agreed objective:

- for brand campaigns, connect attention to qualified reach, watch depth, completion, retention, and recall proxies;
- for performance campaigns, connect attention to traffic, conversion events, codes, links, or advertiser-side attribution when available;
- for sponsorship campaigns, connect attention to creator fit, context, repeat exposure, and audience quality.

## Creator Promotion Reporting

Because creator marketing is part of the inventory, the report should include creator promotion delivery.

Track:

- number of external posts;
- stories;
- Shorts/Reels/TikToks;
- YouTube mentions;
- newsletter mentions;
- X posts;
- livestream mentions;
- pinned links;
- release-day pushes;
- reminder pushes.

Campaign performance should always be interpreted alongside creator promotion.

If creator promotion was weak, the report should say so internally.

If creator promotion was strong, Vanta should use it as proof that the model works.

Creator promotion reporting explains the heat source. It shows whether the campaign succeeded because the creator actually moved people, or underperformed because there was not enough distribution energy behind the property.

## Internal Campaign Review

After every campaign, run an internal review.

Questions:

- Did the creator deliver the content?
- Did the creator promote hard enough?
- Did quality plus promotion create enough heat?
- Did the audience migrate?
- Did viewers become Qualified Viewers?
- Did the advertiser get the audience they expected?
- Did the package price feel too low, fair, or too high?
- Did the service load match the deal size?
- What objection appeared during the sale?
- What reporting did the advertiser care about most?
- Should this advertiser renew?
- Should this package become standardized?
- Should this creator be priced higher next time?
- Did the buyer trust the measurement?
- Did the buyer ask for third-party verification?
- Did the buyer evaluate the campaign as brand, performance, creator, social, CTV, or experimental budget?
- Which internal stakeholder slowed or advanced the deal?

Every campaign should teach the sales system something.

## Renewal Motion

The renewal ask should happen soon after report delivery.

Default renewal structure:

```text
1. Deliver report.
2. Highlight Qualified Attention and strongest audience behavior.
3. Compare results to the advertiser's objective.
4. Recommend the next package.
5. Ask for renewal or expansion.
```

Example:

```text
The campaign delivered [X] Qualified Viewers with [Y] average watch minutes and [Z]% returning viewer behavior. The strongest signal was [specific insight].

For the next campaign, we recommend moving from [pilot/package] into [creator launch/season/category package] because [reason].
```

Do not end a successful report with passive gratitude.

End it with a specific next buy.

## Renewal Categories

### Renew Same Creator

Use when:

- creator fit was strong;
- audience quality was high;
- advertiser liked the association;
- delivery was clean.

### Expand To Season

Use when:

- the advertiser bought a pilot;
- the series is continuing;
- the first campaign created proof;
- the buyer wants more durable association.

### Expand To Category

Use when:

- the buyer cares about a vertical;
- several creators match the buyer;
- Vanta has enough inventory to make a category buy credible.

### Retarget Qualified Viewers

Use when:

- Vanta has enough first-party viewer data;
- the advertiser wants performance;
- campaign policy and privacy requirements allow it.

### Convert To Case Study

Use when:

- the results are strong;
- the brand is recognizable;
- the creator performed well;
- the advertiser is willing to be named.

> Brands like you are buying verified creator attention on Vanta.

## Reporting Integrity

Never fake precision.

Never inflate weak data.

Never hide underdelivery.

Never report signals Vanta did not measure.

The long-term asset is trust.

Qualified Attention only matters if advertisers believe it.

## Third-Party Verification Roadmap

Where commercially practical, Vanta should move toward standards-aligned verification for video advertising.

This may eventually include:

- viewability verification;
- invalid-traffic verification;
- ad delivery verification;
- brand safety and suitability verification;
- standards-aligned attention reporting;
- third-party measurement partner support.

Until those systems exist, sellers must be honest:

> Vanta can report first-party Qualified Attention today. Third-party verification is a future trust layer or a deal-specific integration, not something to imply before it exists.
