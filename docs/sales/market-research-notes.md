# Market Research Notes

This document records outside market evidence that should shape Vanta's sales strategy.

It is not a pitch deck.

It is the grounding layer that prevents Vanta from selling on assumptions alone.

Use this research to sharpen the creator-benefit case, not just the buyer pitch. The strongest evidence should clarify why serious creator attention deserves better packaging, better measurement, and better economics.

Read every source through the thermal-energy model: high-quality creator content plus creator hustle creates heat; Vanta converts that heat into money through advertiser demand, measurement, packaging, sales, reporting, and renewals.

## Research Sources

### IAB Creator Economy Ad Spend And Strategy

Source: https://www.iab.com/insights/2025-creator-economy-ad-spend-strategy-report/

Key findings:

- U.S. creator ad spend is projected to reach $37B in 2025.
- Creator ad spend is projected to reach $44B in 2026.
- Creator ad spend grew from $13.9B in 2021 to $29.5B in 2024.
- Nearly half of creator ad buyers consider creators a must-buy.
- The top challenges include measuring business outcomes, choosing the right creators, measurement, standards, and operational tools.

Vanta implication:

> The market wants creator media, but it is asking for better measurement, better creator selection, and better operating infrastructure. Vanta should sell into those pain points directly.

### IAB Internet Advertising Revenue Report

Source: https://www.iab.com/insights/internet-advertising-revenue-report-full-year-2025/

Key findings:

- U.S. digital advertising revenue reached $294.6B in 2025.
- The market is becoming more performance-driven and AI-powered.
- The report covers video, social, search, commerce media, and the creator economy as core parts of the digital advertising ecosystem.

Vanta implication:

> Vanta is not entering a small or speculative ad market. It is entering a large digital market where buyers already spend heavily, but demand clearer performance proof.

### IAB Digital Video Ad Spend

Source: https://www.iab.com/news/u-s-digital-video-ad-spend-to-surpass-80b-in-2026/

Key findings:

- U.S. digital video ad spend is projected to surpass $80B in 2026.
- Digital video is projected to grow 11% year over year.
- Growth is faster than the total ad market.

Vanta implication:

> Vanta should be sold as premium digital video and creator media inventory, not only as influencer marketing.

### IAB / MRC Attention Measurement Guidelines

Source: https://www.iab.com/guidelines/attention/

Key findings:

- IAB and MRC created guidelines for consistent attention measurement across digital and cross-media environments.
- The guidelines emphasize common definitions, methodological transparency, validation, auditing, and disclosure.
- IAB describes attention as complementary to delivery and outcome metrics, not a standalone replacement for them.
- The marketer playbook warns against treating attention as a binary outcome or universal currency.

Vanta implication:

> Qualified Attention should be positioned as Vanta's transparent attention-quality framework, not as an unexplained black-box score, standalone truth, or universal currency. Sales materials should expose the underlying components and algorithm version, then connect attention to the buyer's stated objective.

### IAB Tech Lab Open Measurement SDK

Source: https://iabtechlab.com/standards/open-measurement-sdk/

Key findings:

- OM SDK is an industry-standard solution for transparency and trust in digital ad measurement.
- It helps advertisers and publishers verify that ads are being seen by real people, are viewable, and deliver value.

Vanta implication:

> Vanta should eventually support third-party verification and standards-aligned video measurement where practical. Until then, sellers must be clear about what Vanta can independently measure and what it cannot yet verify through third parties.

### CreatorIQ State Of Creator Marketing 2025-2026

Source: https://www.creatoriq.com/white-papers/state-of-creator-marketing-trends-2026

Key findings:

- Creator marketing is being shaped by measurement, AI, speed, and brand safety.
- The report is based on more than 1,700 respondents across 17 industries.
- Brands are focused on ROI, paid amplification, creator selection, operational maturity, and brand safety.

Vanta implication:

> Sellers should frame Vanta as an operating system for creator media, not merely another sponsorship broker. The strongest commercial wedge is operational maturity: packaging, measurement, safety, reporting, and renewal discipline.

### WFA / GARM Brand Safety Floor And Suitability Framework

Source: https://wfanet.org/knowledge/item/2022/06/17/GARM-Brand-Safety-Floor--Suitability-Framework-3

Key findings:

- The GARM Brand Safety Floor and Suitability Framework was built to create common definitions and categories for brand safety.
- WFA states that GARM was discontinued on August 8, 2024.

Vanta implication:

> Sellers should not imply that Vanta is operating under an active GARM governance process. Vanta can still use standards-inspired safety and suitability categories because buyers understand that language, but the policy should be described as Vanta's own brand safety and suitability framework.

### Gartner B2B Buying Research

Sources:

- https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-20-gartner-survey-finds-sixty-nine-percent-of-b-two-b-buyers-turn-to-sales-reps-to-validate-ai-generated-insights
- https://www.gartner.com/en/sales/insights/b2b-buying-journey

Key findings:

- Many B2B buyers prefer rep-free or digital self-service buying experiences.
- Buyers still turn to sales reps to validate insights and build confidence at critical points.
- Gartner warns that self-service alone can increase purchase regret when buyers lack the right support.

Vanta implication:

> Vanta sellers should not hoard information behind calls. They should provide clear buyer-enablement materials, then use human selling to create confidence, answer objections, and shape the right package.

## Strategy Confirmed

The research supports these existing Vanta beliefs:

- creator advertising is a real and growing budget category;
- video inventory is a large and growing spend category;
- creator trust and audience fit matter;
- raw impressions are not enough;
- advertisers want more accountability;
- fragmented creator buying creates pain;
- brand safety and suitability must be addressed upfront;
- operational packaging can be a competitive advantage.

## Strategy Tightened

The research also forces several sharper rules:

- Do not sell Qualified Attention as an audited industry currency until Vanta has earned that level of validation.
- Do sell Qualified Attention as a transparent, component-based internal framework for attention quality that complements delivery and outcome metrics.
- Do not rely on platform scale claims when the early inventory depends on creator-imported audience.
- Do make campaign deliverables, creator promotion, brand safety, and reporting explicit before launch.
- Do not expect sellers to win by charm alone. Buyers need enablement artifacts they can circulate internally.
- Do not let business-outcome buyers push Vanta into vague performance promises. Define the outcome metric and attribution method before the deal.
- Do not imply active GARM participation. Use standards-inspired brand safety language and define Vanta's own safety policy.
