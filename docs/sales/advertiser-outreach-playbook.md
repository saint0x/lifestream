# Advertiser Outreach Playbook

## Outreach Principle

Advertiser outreach should be high-volume, segmented, and specific.

Do not pitch Vanta generically.

Pitch the match between:

- the advertiser's buyer;
- the creator's audience;
- the exclusive series;
- and Vanta's Qualified Attention measurement.

The best first message should make the advertiser think:

> This audience already looks like a customer segment I care about.

## Prospecting Sources

Build advertiser lists from evidence of existing spend.

High-signal sources include:

- brands sponsoring similar creators;
- brands sponsoring podcasts in the same niche;
- brands running YouTube integrations;
- brands running TikTok or Instagram creator campaigns;
- brands advertising in newsletters;
- brands sponsoring events in the category;
- brands buying CTV, streaming, or digital video;
- brands already spending on affiliate or performance marketing;
- brands mentioned organically by the creator;
- brands the audience already discusses, buys, or recommends.

The best early prospects are not random companies.

They are companies already demonstrating willingness to pay for creator attention.

## Brand Affinity Map

For every creator or series, build a brand affinity map.

Include:

- creator category;
- audience identity;
- likely buyer personas;
- products already used by the audience;
- competitor creators with sponsors;
- existing brand mentions;
- ideal sponsor categories;
- excluded sponsor categories;
- premium sponsor targets;
- mid-market sponsor targets;
- low-touch sponsor targets.

This map becomes the outbound list.

## Priority Verticals

Strong early advertiser categories include:

- software and AI tools;
- developer tools;
- business and finance products;
- fitness and wellness;
- apparel and fashion;
- outdoor gear;
- sports equipment;
- automotive products;
- gaming hardware and software;
- food and beverage;
- education and course platforms;
- creator tools;
- travel;
- consumer fintech;
- health-adjacent products with appropriate compliance review.

Prioritize categories where creator trust materially improves conversion.

## Outreach Volume

Advertiser acquisition should mirror creator acquisition discipline.

Vanta should maintain a large top-of-funnel advertiser motion, while separating premium targets from low-ticket targets.

Suggested weekly operating rhythm:

- 200-500 advertiser prospects researched;
- 100-250 outbound messages sent;
- 15-40 qualified replies or follow-ups;
- 5-15 serious sales conversations;
- 2-5 proposals;
- 0-2 closed pilots or renewals.

These numbers are directional and should be adjusted once actual conversion data exists.

The principle is strict:

> Do not rely on a few glamorous brand conversations to carry the revenue pipeline.

Cold outbound is crowded.

The seller should assume reply rates will be modest unless the message is highly specific.

Generic AI-written outreach will be ignored.

The best outbound message should prove:

- the seller knows what the advertiser sells;
- the seller understands the buyer's customer;
- the creator/category match is specific;
- the package is real;
- the measurement story is credible;
- the next step is low-friction.

## Touchpoint Discipline

Do not stop after one email.

Use a concise sequence:

```text
Day 1: specific opener
Day 3: quantified creator/category fit
Day 7: inventory sheet or example package
Day 14: objection-led follow-up
Day 21: clean breakup or lower-friction CTA
```

Each touch should add information.

Do not send five versions of the same email.

## Buyer Enablement Packet

When a buyer shows interest, send a packet they can forward internally.

The packet should include:

- one-page Vanta overview;
- creator/category inventory sheet;
- package options;
- Qualified Attention explanation;
- sample campaign report;
- brand safety controls;
- pricing range or proposal;
- next-step recommendation.

The goal is to help the buyer sell Vanta inside their own company.

## Default Cold Email

Subject options:

```text
Sponsor [Creator/Series] on Vanta
Verified creator attention in [category]
[Category] audience sponsorship
```

Body:

```text
Hey [Name],

Vanta is launching exclusive premium programming with creators whose audiences are already strong fits for [brand/category].

[Creator/Series] is building [short description], and their audience maps closely to [specific buyer/audience reason].

The difference from a normal creator post is that Vanta measures Qualified Attention: not just raw views, but viewers who actually watch, return, and engage around the exclusive series.

We are opening a limited number of launch sponsorships for this property and thought [Brand] could be a strong fit.

Worth a quick look?
```

```text
Quick follow-up here.

For [Brand], the likely fit is [creator/category/package] because [specific reason].

Happy to send the one-page inventory sheet if useful.
```

## Discovery Questions

In sales calls, quickly identify:

- who they currently sponsor;
- what channels they already buy;
- average campaign size;
- target customer;
- performance metric;
- brand safety constraints;
- category exclusivity needs;
- creative restrictions;
- reporting requirements;
- renewal criteria;
- decision timeline.
- who else must approve the buy;
- what internal objection could kill the deal;
- whether the budget is brand, creator, social, CTV, experimental, affiliate, or performance.

Do not over-explain Vanta before understanding the buyer's current spend.

## Qualification

A prospect is qualified when they have at least three of:

- existing creator/influencer/podcast/video spend;
- clear target customer overlap;
- budget above the relevant lane floor;
- active campaign need in the next 90 days;
- category fit with available creator inventory;
- willingness to evaluate Qualified Attention;
- decision-maker access;
- no obvious brand safety conflict.

## Objection Handling

### "How many users does Vanta have?"

Answer:

> The buy is not generic platform reach. The buy is creator-imported audience around exclusive programming. We measure how many viewers the creator brings, how deeply they watch, and how much Qualified Attention the campaign receives.

### "Why not just sponsor the creator directly?"

Answer:

> You can, but direct creator sponsorships are often fragmented and hard to measure. Vanta turns the creator's audience into a structured media property with exclusive content, campaign packaging, and Qualified Attention reporting.

### "Is this influencer marketing?"

Answer:

> It overlaps with creator marketing, but the product is closer to sponsoring premium creator programming. The creator is the distribution channel; the Vanta series is the media property.

### "What if the campaign underperforms?"

Answer:

> Early packages should define expected delivery, reporting, and makegood rules before launch. We will not promise fake certainty. We will show measured attention and optimize against it.

## Daily Sales Discipline

Every sales day should produce one or more of:

- new qualified advertiser prospects;
- outbound messages;
- follow-ups;
- booked meetings;
- proposals;
- closed campaigns;
- campaign reports;
- renewal asks;
- case-study assets.

Activity is not the goal.

Revenue and renewals are the goal.
