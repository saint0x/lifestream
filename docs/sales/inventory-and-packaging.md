# Inventory And Packaging

Packaging should make creator attention easier to buy without making the creator feel commoditized. The best packages give advertisers clarity while giving creators better economics, cleaner expectations, and a stronger reason to keep building premium programming on Vanta.

## Packaging Principle

Vanta should turn fragmented creator attention into structured media inventory.

The buyer should not have to decipher every creator, episode, placement, metric, and price from scratch.

Vanta should package inventory into clear offers that are easy to buy, easy to explain internally, and easy to report after the campaign.

Packaging should make the thermal-energy model legible:

```text
Creator quality + creator hustle
-> audience heat
-> measurable attention
-> sponsor-ready inventory
-> money
```

If a package does not identify where the heat comes from, how it will be concentrated, and how Vanta will convert it, the package is not clear enough.

## What Can Be Sold

Vanta can sell multiple inventory types.

### Creator Inventory

Advertising tied directly to a creator.

Examples:

- creator page sponsorship;
- creator series sponsorship;
- episode sponsorship;
- creator-read integration;
- creator social promotion tied to the Vanta release;
- live event sponsorship;
- launch-week sponsorship.

### Series Inventory

Advertising tied to one recurring programming property.

Examples:

- presented-by sponsorship;
- title-card sponsorship;
- pre-roll;
- mid-roll;
- post-roll;
- product integration;
- branded challenge;
- episode recap sponsorship;
- season finale sponsorship.

### Category Inventory

Advertising tied to a niche or vertical.

Examples:

- technology category sponsorship;
- outdoor creator slate;
- fitness transformation slate;
- gaming live-event bundle;
- founder/build-in-public category.

Category inventory is strategically important because it lets brands buy an audience thesis instead of only one creator.

### Platform Inventory

Advertising tied to Vanta-owned surfaces.

Examples:

- homepage feature;
- discovery placement;
- category page sponsorship;
- newsletter or notification placement;
- search placement;
- featured premiere;
- sponsored collection.

Platform inventory should become more valuable as creator aggregation increases.

## Default Product Menu

### 1. Creator Launch Package

Purpose:

> Help a brand sponsor the launch of a creator's new Vanta property.

Includes:

- launch-week sponsorship;
- show-page brand placement;
- pre-roll or intro placement;
- creator social promotion requirement;
- Qualified Attention report;
- renewal option.

Best for:

- mid-market brands;
- first-time Vanta advertisers;
- creator-aligned DTC companies;
- brands testing a niche audience.

### 2. Season Sponsorship

Purpose:

> Let a brand own a full season of a premium creator series.

Includes:

- sponsorship across all episodes in a season;
- presented-by placement;
- optional creator read;
- show-page placement;
- launch and finale moments;
- campaign recap;
- category exclusivity option.

Best for:

- big brands;
- agencies;
- brands that want durable association with a creator or niche.

### 3. Category Ownership

Purpose:

> Let a brand become the dominant sponsor of a creator category for a fixed period.

Includes:

- multiple creators or shows;
- category page placement;
- cross-series brand presence;
- optional exclusivity against competing brands;
- aggregate Qualified Attention reporting;
- case-study option.

Best for:

- larger brands;
- advertisers that already buy sponsorships at scale;
- brands trying to own a cultural or niche audience.

### 4. Qualified Attention Pilot

Purpose:

> Give advertisers a low-friction way to test Vanta's measurement and audience quality.

Includes:

- fixed campaign window;
- one creator or one small creator bundle;
- standard placement;
- no heavy custom work;
- Qualified Viewer and Verified Viewer Score report;
- renewal recommendation.

Best for:

- $5,000-$25,000 advertisers;
- performance-minded brands;
- early proof-building.

### 5. Self-Serve Starter Buy

Purpose:

> Allow smaller advertisers to buy without consuming high-touch sales time.

Includes:

- fixed placement menu;
- fixed creative specs;
- standard campaign window;
- templated report;
- no custom brand strategy.

Best for:

- $1,000-$5,000 advertisers;
- inbound buyers;
- local or niche brands.

## Service-Level Rules

### High-Touch

Reserved for:

```text
$50,000+
```

Allowed:

- custom deck;
- custom proposal;
- live pitch;
- campaign strategy;
- bespoke recap;
- category exclusivity negotiation.

### Productized Sales

Reserved for:

```text
$5,000-$50,000
```

Allowed:

- standard deck;
- standard package;
- limited customization;
- templated recap with campaign-specific numbers;
- one renewal discussion.

### Low-Touch

Reserved for:

```text
Below $5,000
```

Allowed:

- fixed menu;
- standard creative requirements;
- automated or templated recap;
- no custom strategy;
- no special reporting;
- no founder calls unless strategically useful.

## Creator Promotion Requirement

Creator promotion is part of the inventory.

Advertisers are not only buying placement inside Vanta. They are buying the creator's ability to mobilize attention toward the Vanta property.

They are buying the creator's heat: the combination of high-quality programming and active promotion.

Each sold package should specify creator promotion commitments, such as:

- number of external posts;
- number of stories;
- newsletter mentions;
- pinned link duration;
- pre-release announcement;
- release-day push;
- post-release reminder;
- livestream mention;
- clip distribution.

The creator's marketing effort is not separate from campaign performance.

It is a core input.

Without creator promotion, there may be content but not enough heat. Without content quality, there may be noise but not valuable thermal energy. Packages should require both.

## Inventory Quality Gate

Do not sell inventory merely because it exists.

Inventory becomes advertiser-ready when Vanta can explain:

- who the audience is;
- why the creator matters;
- why the content is exclusive;
- how creator quality and promotion create heat;
- what the advertiser receives;
- what the creator will promote;
- what Qualified Attention will be measured;
- what the price is;
- and what the post-campaign report will contain.

If those answers are unclear, the inventory is not packaged yet.

## Inventory Transparency Standard

Advertisers increasingly care where their ads actually run.

Every package should be able to answer:

- exact creator or creators;
- exact series or category;
- placement format;
- placement timing;
- content adjacency;
- brand safety exclusions;
- creator approval or usage rights;
- campaign window;
- reporting window;
- whether promotion is guaranteed or best-efforts;
- what happens if content or promotion is delayed.

If Vanta cannot answer these questions, the package is not ready for serious buyers.

## Brand Safety And Suitability

Brand safety should be defined before launch.

Vanta should use clear safety and suitability categories that buyers understand, but should describe them as Vanta's own policy.

Do not imply that Vanta is governed by an active GARM process.

Each package should specify:

- prohibited categories;
- sensitive topics;
- competitor conflicts;
- claim restrictions;
- product category restrictions;
- creator conduct concerns;
- approval rights;
- takedown or replacement process;
- makegood rules if adjacency materially violates the agreement.

Suitability is not identical for every brand.

Some brands can sponsor edgy creator programming.

Some cannot.

The sales team should clarify the advertiser's risk tolerance early rather than discovering it after creative is live.
