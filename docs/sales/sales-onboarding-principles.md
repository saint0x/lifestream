# Sales Onboarding Principles

## Seller Mission

The seller's mission is to convert Vanta's exclusive creator programming into advertiser revenue in a way that also strengthens the creator's media property.

Do not confuse this with selling generic media.

Do not confuse this with selling influencer posts.

Do not confuse this with selling a speculative platform vision.

The practical job is:

> Put advertiser capital against verified creator-imported attention, help the creator earn from that attention, then turn proof into renewals.

The thermal-energy model is the seller's simplest internal operating frame:

```text
High-quality creator content
+ creator hustle, advertising, and promotion
= heat

Heat concentrated into real audience attention
= thermal energy

Vanta conversion engine
= money
```

Vanta gets paid because it converts that thermal energy into advertiser revenue. The creator gets paid because their quality and hustle become measurable, packageable, and sponsorable.

## The Core Sentence

Every seller should be able to say this clearly:

> Vanta helps brands sponsor exclusive creator programming watched by audiences the creators actively bring to the platform, then reports the quality of that attention so creators and advertisers can both understand the value being created.

This sentence contains the whole model:

- exclusive programming;
- creator-imported audience;
- brand sponsorship;
- measured attention quality;
- better reporting than scattered sponsorships.

## What To Lead With

Lead with the buyer's commercial problem.

Most advertisers are not suffering from a lack of places to spend money.

They are suffering from:

- fragmented creator deals;
- uncertain attention quality;
- weak measurement;
- passive impressions;
- unclear audience trust;
- difficulty finding culturally relevant creator inventory;
- and too much manual sponsorship management.

Vanta's answer is:

> We turn creator attention into structured, measurable, sponsorable media inventory.

## Buyer Enablement Is Part Of Sales

Modern B2B buyers often want to research before or alongside talking to a seller.

Do not treat this as a threat.

Use it.

Every serious buyer should be able to receive a clean enablement packet:

- one-page Vanta overview;
- creator or category inventory sheet;
- Qualified Attention explanation;
- example campaign report;
- package menu;
- brand safety and suitability policy;
- creator promotion commitments;
- pricing range or proposal;
- clear next step.

The salesperson's job is not to hide information until a call.

The salesperson's job is to make the buyer confident enough to move.

## What Not To Lead With

Do not open with:

- AI infrastructure;
- product architecture;
- recommendation systems;
- the full platform roadmap;
- creator philosophy;
- marketplace theory;
- every future feature;
- or a complicated monetization diagram.

Those details may matter later.

The opening must be simple:

> This is premium creator programming, with measurable attention, sold as structured sponsorship inventory.

Or, internally:

> The creator creates the heat. Vanta converts the heat into money.

## The Buyer Must Understand The Difference

A normal influencer post is often:

```text
Brand pays creator
-> Creator posts
-> Brand receives shallow metrics
-> Campaign ends
```

Vanta is trying to build:

```text
Brand sponsors creator property
-> Creator drives audience to exclusive programming
-> Creator quality + promotion creates thermal energy
-> Audience watches in a measurable environment
-> Vanta reports Qualified Attention
-> Brand renews or expands
-> Creator earns more
-> Creator promotes harder
```

The seller must make this distinction feel obvious.

## The Unit Of Value

The unit of value is not:

> a view

The unit of value is:

> qualified attention around a creator media property.

This allows the seller to defend premium pricing.

The buyer should leave the conversation believing:

- Vanta's audience is not random;
- the creator actively moved that audience;
- the content environment is deeper than a feed;
- the inventory can be bought cleanly;
- and the reporting will be more serious than a screenshot of platform analytics.

## Seller Discipline

High-powered sellers will naturally want flexibility.

Flexibility is useful.

Unbounded customization is dangerous.

The seller should protect these constraints:

- do not sell inventory that cannot be delivered;
- do not promise metrics Vanta cannot measure;
- do not let low-budget buyers demand high-touch labor;
- do not discount without getting strategic value back;
- do not pitch Vanta as already having massive native distribution if the campaign depends on creator-imported traffic;
- do not bury the creator's promotion responsibility;
- do not let a brand think they are buying passive platform reach when they are buying creator-mobilized attention.
- do not force every buyer through a high-friction sales process when a clear buyer packet would move them faster.

## Output Standard

Sales activity is not success.

Success is:

- advertiser budget committed;
- campaign launched;
- creator promotion delivered;
- Qualified Attention measured;
- report delivered;
- renewal or expansion pursued;
- creator and Vanta both earn.

Everything else is an intermediate variable.

## Plain-English Test

Before a seller enters a call, they should be able to answer:

- Why this brand?
- Why this creator or category?
- Why Vanta instead of direct sponsorship?
- Why exclusive programming matters?
- What attention will be measured?
- What package should they buy?
- What price should they pay?
- What objection is most likely?
- What is the next action if they say yes?

If the seller cannot answer those questions, the account is not ready for a serious pitch.
