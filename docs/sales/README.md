# Vanta Sales Bible

This folder is the operating system for selling Vanta's advertiser inventory.

The business docs explain why Vanta should exist. These sales docs explain how Vanta should package, pitch, sell, report, and renew advertising against exclusive creator programming.

Every sales motion should preserve the creator-benefit thesis. Vanta is self-interested, but the sale only works long term when the creator gets a better economic structure, clearer proof of audience value, and more leverage from the work they are already making.

## Core Sales Thesis

Vanta does not sell generic impressions.

Vanta sells:

> Verified creator-imported attention around exclusive premium programming that helps creators turn audience trust into durable media value.

The strict mental model:

> Vanta is an engine that converts thermal energy into money.

Creators create heat when they do two things at the same time:

- make high-quality exclusive content;
- hustle, advertise, distribute, and promote that high-quality content to their own audience.

The combination of quality and hustle creates thermal energy: real audience demand, movement, attention, return behavior, and advertiser-relevant proof.

Vanta converts that thermal energy into money through packaging, measurement, sales, reporting, and renewals. Vanta gets paid for operating the conversion engine, and creators get paid because their heat becomes commercially legible.

That means the advertiser is buying a combination of:

- creator trust;
- exclusive content;
- measurable viewer attention;
- audience migration from the creator's existing channels;
- a repeatable media property rather than a one-off post;
- and reporting through Qualified Attention.

## Operating Rule

The sales organization should always protect the core flywheel:

```text
Creator brings audience
-> Audience watches exclusive programming
-> Creator quality + creator hustle creates thermal energy
-> Vanta verifies Qualified Attention
-> Advertiser pays premium access
-> Creator earns meaningful revenue
-> Creator promotes harder
-> More creators join
```

Anything that does not strengthen this loop should be treated as secondary.
