# Vanta Plus

Vanta Plus is not "premium Vanta."

Vanta Plus is the operating system for a creator who has become a studio.

Free Vanta helps creators publish, distribute, build audience behavior, and monetize qualified attention. Vanta Plus gives serious creators leverage: the production, intelligence, automation, and business infrastructure that normally appears only once a creator is large enough to hire producers, assistants, analysts, coordinators, researchers, and operations staff.

The creator-benefit framing matters. Vanta Plus should not ask creators to pay for a slightly nicer dashboard. It should help them become more capable media companies.

At the simplest level:

```text
Vanta Free = Run your channel.
Vanta Plus = Run your studio.
```

## Strategic Role

The core Vanta platform should remain free and frictionless wherever possible.

Free Vanta creates reach, audience migration, qualified attention, advertising inventory, creator monetization, and platform-level media value. Vanta Plus should sit above that foundation as an optional subscription layer for creators who need deeper operational leverage.

That distinction keeps the business model clean:

- Free Vanta monetizes the economic activity around creator content.
- Vanta Plus helps creators produce, operate, understand, grow, and monetize at a higher level.
- Free Vanta should make the creator more valuable to advertisers.
- Vanta Plus should help the creator behave like a real studio.

The product should not feel like a tax on participation. It should feel like the point where a creator says:

> I am no longer just uploading content. I am running a media company.

## The Five Jobs

Internally, Vanta Plus should be judged against five jobs:

```text
CREATE -> OPERATE -> UNDERSTAND -> GROW -> MONETIZE
```

Every major Plus feature should materially improve at least one of those jobs.

That rule prevents the product from becoming a generic AI bundle. The goal is not to sell creators disconnected features such as captions, thumbnails, or more storage. The goal is to sell infrastructure previously available only to creators with a team.

## Studio Assistant

The Studio Assistant is the interface to Vanta Plus.

It should not merely be a chatbot. It should understand the creator's shows, episodes, audience, publishing history, collaborators, connected contracts and permissions, calendar, goals, external platforms, sponsor obligations, and unfinished ideas.

A creator should be able to say:

> I want Season 2 ready by October. Figure out what needs to happen.

Vanta Plus should translate that into a production plan, schedule, task list, research packet, reminders, collaborator assignments, platform actions, and publishing sequence.

The important product distinction is:

```text
Conversation -> execution
```

not:

```text
Conversation -> text
```

## Production Infrastructure

Vanta Plus should be useful before the camera ever turns on.

It can support:

- Concept development
- Series and season architecture
- Episode planning
- Research packets
- Treatments
- Shot lists
- Production schedules
- Guest preparation
- Interview research
- Location planning
- Asset requirements
- Post-production checklists
- Release calendars

One strong product primitive is a `Prepare Episode` action.

For an upcoming episode, Vanta Plus could automatically assemble the production packet: premise, intended audience, research, guest notes, scenes, interview questions, deliverables, schedule, collaborators, sponsor constraints, promotional angles, and release checklist.

The benefit is straightforward: creators spend more energy making better work and less energy reconstructing the same operating process every week.

## Creator Automations

Creator automations may become one of the strongest reasons to pay for Plus.

Creators should be able to install automations almost like hiring tiny employees:

- Weekly Studio Report summarizes audience, revenue, content performance, production status, and important changes.
- Release Manager coordinates the work surrounding a new episode.
- Audience Intelligence extracts themes, questions, requests, and objections from audience activity.
- Sponsor Tracker manages sponsorship obligations, deliverables, deadlines, and renewal windows.
- Guest Researcher prepares dossiers before interviews.
- Content Librarian organizes footage, clips, episodes, transcripts, and reusable moments.
- Opportunity Scout surfaces unusual growth, content, partnership, and business opportunities from connected data.
- Series Analyst watches episode performance and recommends adjustments to future episodes.

Eventually, creators should be able to describe a custom automation in natural language and have Vanta Plus build the operating loop around it.

## Studio Memory

Studio Memory can become one of Vanta Plus's deepest advantages.

Vanta Plus should maintain persistent institutional memory for the creator's studio. It should know what the creator has made, what they have said, what worked, what failed, which topics recur, which collaborators contributed, which sponsors were involved, how audiences responded, what footage remains unused, which ideas were abandoned, and which creative preferences keep appearing over time.

After three years, Vanta Plus should not feel like a generic AI tool with integrations. It should feel like someone who has worked inside the creator's production company for three years.

That memory compounds:

- Better episode planning
- Better follow-up ideas
- Better sponsor context
- Better audience understanding
- Better archive retrieval
- Better collaborator coordination
- Better creative continuity

The creator's studio history becomes an active asset instead of a trail of disconnected files and forgotten decisions.

## Creator Intelligence

Serious creators need more than prettier view dashboards.

Vanta Plus should operate like an analyst. It should answer questions such as:

- Why did this episode outperform?
- Which subjects turn casual viewers into recurring viewers?
- Which guests brought valuable audiences?
- What percentage of my audience watches multiple series?
- Which 15 minutes of my last five episodes generated the most downstream engagement?
- What should I make more of?
- Which formats create the most qualified attention?
- Which promotional channels bring the viewers most likely to return?

The product should generate reports, not merely display charts.

This matters because Vanta's broader business model depends on creators producing high-quality content and creating heat around that content through aggressive promotion, collaboration, and audience development. Better intelligence helps creators decide where to focus that effort.

## Audience CRM

Creators increasingly have audiences but very little audience infrastructure.

Vanta Plus can create a private intelligence layer around the creator's community:

- Superfans
- Recurring viewers
- Paying supporters
- High-value commenters
- Community clusters
- Audience interests
- Churn risk
- Conversion cohorts
- Launch targets

Eventually, a creator should be able to ask:

> Find the 500 viewers most likely to care about my new documentary and create a launch strategy for them.

That is not a vanity dashboard. That is real studio infrastructure.

## Business Operations

Once a creator becomes a one-person media company, non-creative work expands quickly.

Vanta Plus can help manage:

- Sponsorship pipelines
- Deliverables
- Invoices
- Revenue reporting
- Expenses
- Collaborator payments
- Rights and usage records
- Production budgets
- Business calendars
- Document organization
- Administrative workflows

Vanta does not need to replace every external business tool. In many cases, it should connect to systems such as accounting, contract, payment, storage, calendar, and communication tools, then become the intelligence and orchestration layer above them.

## Deal And Sponsorship Infrastructure

For sufficiently large creators, deal infrastructure alone could justify Vanta Plus.

Creators need a lightweight deal desk: sponsorship pipeline, historical rates, campaign performance, available inventory, obligations, deliverables, renewal dates, and comparable deal context.

For example, Vanta Plus should eventually help a creator understand:

> Brand X offered $18,000 for this integration. Your comparable integrations generated materially stronger engagement than the category benchmark, and your next two episodes are already sold. Counter higher.

The deeper point is that Vanta Plus should help creators negotiate and operate like actual media companies.

This also reinforces the main Vanta model. Creators create heat by making high-quality content and promoting it with real discipline. Vanta turns that qualified attention into money through advertising, sponsorship, marketplace infrastructure, and measurement. Vanta Plus gives the creator more machinery for producing the heat in the first place.

## Content And IP Library

Vanta Plus should treat everything creators produce as intellectual property, not files.

It can index:

- Footage
- Transcripts
- Clips
- Artwork
- Music
- Guests
- Topics
- Projects
- Published material
- Unused material
- Sponsorship references
- Collaborator contributions

A creator should be able to ask:

> Find every time I talked about independent filmmaking in the last two years.

Or:

> There is enough unused footage from the Jamaica shoot for another episode. Build one.

The archive becomes active inventory.

## Distribution Intelligence

Vanta Plus should not simply tell creators to post everywhere.

It should understand what should go where, how the packaging should differ, what should remain Vanta-exclusive, what should become promotional media elsewhere, when something should publish, and what objective each piece serves.

That makes Vanta the control plane for the creator's wider media footprint, even when a promotional asset ultimately appears somewhere else.

The strategic sequence should remain:

```text
High-quality Vanta property
-> aggressive external promotion
-> qualified attention
-> stronger advertiser value
-> more creator revenue
```

## Collaboration And Virtual Studio

Serious creators eventually stop being literally one person.

Vanta Plus should grow with the creator's team. A creator should be able to invite an editor, producer, manager, researcher, agent, cinematographer, designer, or collaborator, then give each person appropriate access to projects, assets, reports, workflows, and decisions.

This matters because Vanta already benefits from visible credits and collaborator graphs. When creators work together directly on the platform, each project can expose more connected people, more clickable credits, more adjacent audiences, and more paths for qualified attention to move across the network.

Vanta Plus should make that collaboration operationally easier:

- Shared production plans
- Role-based project access
- Collaborator assignments
- Centralized assets
- Decision history
- Credit tracking
- Cross-project context
- Team-facing reports

The more serious the collaboration becomes, the more valuable the creator's studio memory becomes.

## Studio Health

The recurring product should be anchored by the Vanta Plus Studio Brief.

Every Monday morning, the creator receives:

```text
Your Studio This Week
```

The brief should cover revenue, audience growth, releases, production status, sponsorship obligations, unusual performance changes, opportunities, problems requiring attention, deadlines, and the three to five highest-leverage actions for the week.

This makes Vanta Plus habitual. It gives creators a weekly operating rhythm rather than another tool they only remember when something is already disorganized.

## Product Standard

The long-term position is not selling creators AI.

Vanta Plus sells the feeling and function of a real studio team sitting behind the creator: producer, researcher, analyst, archivist, business manager, operations coordinator, and deal desk.

The creator should feel more capable after using it. They should make better work, understand their audience more clearly, coordinate collaborators more easily, negotiate from stronger context, and compound the value of everything they publish.

That is the subscription worth building.
