# Vanta Business Proposal

This folder contains the core Vanta business proposal and creator-economy thesis, split into focused documents for easier review and reuse.

The proposal is commercially self-interested, but it should be read through a creator-benefit lens: Vanta exists to help creators turn excellent work and trusted audiences into premium media properties, measurable attention, and stronger monetization without forcing them to abandon the platforms they already use.

## Documents

- [Platform Thesis](./platform-thesis.md) covers the executive summary, product concept, creator positioning, exclusivity, audience migration, and creator pages.
- [Revenue Model](./revenue-model.md) covers the advertising marketplace, platform advertising, audience support, free-access model, and frictionless viewing.
- [Qualified Attention](./qualified-attention.md) covers the Qualified Viewer system, raw views versus verified attention, bot filtering, and premium attention pricing.
- [Advertising Marketplace](./advertising-marketplace.md) covers the advertiser portal, Vanta as a media sales organization, marketplace bootstrapping, launch sequencing, and the strategic conclusion.
- [Vanta Plus](./vanta-plus.md) covers the forthcoming subscription offering for serious creators: studio assistance, production infrastructure, automations, studio memory, creator intelligence, audience CRM, business operations, sponsorship infrastructure, IP libraries, distribution intelligence, collaboration, and studio health.

## Core Thesis

Vanta gives internet creators an HBO-quality distribution environment, helps them package their audience trust into a real media property, gives advertisers direct access to highly qualified creator audiences, and monetizes the infrastructure connecting the two.

The platform is designed to monetize the economic activity surrounding content rather than requiring the content itself to sit behind a paywall.

Vanta Plus extends that thesis without replacing it: free Vanta helps creators run the channel and monetize qualified attention, while Vanta Plus helps creators who have become studios operate with more leverage.
