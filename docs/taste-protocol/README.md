# Vanta Taste Protocol

This archive contains the Executive Director Questionnaire for building Vanta's V1 Taste and Production Archive.

The goal of the protocol is to extract elite creative judgment into a structured taste layer for Vanta's automated media-production system. It is not just a list of camera settings, editing tricks, or production conventions. It is a system for understanding how an elite creative director decides what should happen, when it should happen, why it should happen, and when the normal rule should be broken.

## Documents

- [01 Foundation](./01-foundation.md): purpose, creative constitution, and taste hierarchy.
- [02 Story And Openings](./02-story-and-openings.md): story extraction from raw footage and first-minute logic.
- [03 Shot And Camera Grammar](./03-shot-and-camera-grammar.md): shot evaluation, shot selection, coverage, lenses, camera position, movement, blocking, composition, and close-ups.
- [04 Editing And Performance](./04-editing-and-performance.md): cuts, edit density, rhythm, removal discipline, performance, and dialogue.
- [05 Audio And Image Language](./05-audio-and-image-language.md): silence, music, sound design, audio quality, lighting, color, texture, aspect ratio, continuity, transitions, geography, B-roll, inserts, locations, production design, wardrobe, titles, and on-screen information.
- [06 Time, Truth, Identity, And Genre](./06-time-truth-identity-genre.md): montage, time manipulation, documentary truth, creator identity, genre profiles, emotion profiles, comedy, suspense, emotional peaks, and imperfection.
- [07 Recovery, Attention, And Series Systems](./07-recovery-attention-series.md): weak-footage recovery, missing shots, repetition, viewer attention, runtime, episodes, series identity, and Vanta house taste.
- [08 Taste Library Implementation](./08-taste-library-implementation.md): references, anti-references, rule confidence, exceptions, decision trees, thresholds, ambiguity, multi-version editing, taste drift, final QC, answer schema, and V1 output.

## Core Objective

The ultimate objective is that an intelligent production system should be able to consult this archive and reason:

> Given what is happening in this footage, what would an elite Vanta executive director do next?
