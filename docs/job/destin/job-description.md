# VANTA
## Chief Content & Culture Officer / Head of Studio

Role: Chief Content & Culture Officer / Head of Studio  
Executive: Destin  
Reports to: CEO  
Function: Content, Creative Direction, Creator Acquisition, Studio, Culture & Partnerships

---

## Role Mission

Destin is responsible for building and directing Vanta's creative universe.

His primary mandate is simple:

> Ensure Vanta continuously has exceptional original and exclusive programming that people cannot get anywhere else.

He owns both sides of that equation:

1. Acquire great content by identifying and signing creators, filmmakers, directors, and cultural talent.
2. Create great content through Vanta's internal creative and production capabilities.

Beyond individual productions, Destin is responsible for codifying Vanta's taste: turning the creative instincts, principles, standards, and decision-making processes that produce exceptional media into a systematic Taste Protocol that can be understood and executed by humans and AI systems.

As Vanta grows, Destin becomes the head of Vanta Studios, overseeing the company's internal production organization, creative talent, production infrastructure, and overall creative standard.

---

# I. ORIGINAL & EXCLUSIVE PROGRAMMING

Destin's central responsibility is building Vanta's programming slate.

The objective is not simply to acquire "content." It is to create a catalog of distinctive, culturally relevant, high-quality series that give audiences a reason to specifically watch Vanta.

Destin owns the pipeline from:

Discovery → Development → Acquisition → Production → Release

Programming can enter Vanta through several paths.

### 1. Established Creator Acquisition

Identify creators, filmmakers, personalities, artists, and cultural figures with existing audiences or demonstrated creative ability.

Develop relationships with those creators and structure opportunities for them to produce:

- Vanta-exclusive series
- Vanta-exclusive films
- documentaries
- recurring programs
- experimental formats
- limited series
- live programming
- special projects
- cultural programming

Destin owns the creative relationship with these creators and works with the CEO on the commercial structure of significant agreements.

The goal is to create programming that brings both great media and existing cultural gravity into the Vanta ecosystem.

---

### 2. Emerging Talent / "Draft Pick" Program

Destin should continuously search for people who demonstrate exceptional taste, perspective, storytelling ability, visual instinct, personality, or cultural understanding, even if they do not yet possess a traditional filmmaking portfolio.

Vanta should be willing to discover talent before the rest of the industry recognizes it.

Destin may effectively draft these creators.

Once selected, Destin works directly with them to:

- develop their creative voice;
- develop a viable series concept;
- establish production standards;
- improve cinematography and storytelling;
- develop recurring formats;
- review footage and cuts;
- coach creative decision-making;
- apply the Vanta Taste Protocol;
- and ultimately produce Vanta-exclusive programming.

The objective is to build an internal talent development pipeline capable of turning promising creative people into exceptional directors and creators.

---

### 3. Vanta Original Productions

Destin may personally originate, direct, produce, or creatively oversee Vanta programming.

These projects become Vanta Originals and serve several purposes:

- create flagship programming;
- establish Vanta's creative identity;
- experiment with new formats;
- demonstrate the capabilities of Vanta's production system;
- establish benchmarks for external creators;
- and continuously push the company's creative standard forward.

Destin has authority to propose new productions whenever he believes Vanta should create something rather than acquire it.

---

# II. VANTA TASTE PROTOCOL

Destin is the principal architect and owner of the Vanta Taste Protocol.

The Taste Protocol is Vanta's codified creative intelligence.

It should answer a much deeper question than:

> "How should we edit a Vanta video?"

It should answer:

> "How does Vanta make creative decisions?"

The protocol should encode the principles necessary for an intelligent creative system to take unfamiliar raw material and make sophisticated decisions about how that material should become exceptional media.

The internal benchmark is intentionally ambitious:

> Give the system twenty minutes of ordinary raw footage and enough creative intelligence to discover the best possible film hiding inside it.

The Taste Protocol therefore should not become a rigid collection of rules such as "cut every X seconds," "always use this transition," or "always use this color grade."

That would create formulaic media.

Instead, it should provide enough context, principles, examples, judgment criteria, and creative reasoning that a competent AI model—or human director—can intelligently determine what a specific piece of footage needs.

### Taste Protocol Domains

Destin should progressively codify Vanta's philosophy around areas including:

Story
- narrative construction
- tension
- pacing
- character
- information density
- emotional arcs
- beginnings and endings
- narrative payoff

Editing
- shot selection
- cutting philosophy
- rhythm

- silence
- transitions
- compression
- when not to cut

Cinematography
- framing
- composition
- movement
- lens choices
- lighting
- visual hierarchy
- environmental storytelling

Sound
- dialogue
- ambience
- music
- silence
- sound design
- sonic transitions

Visual Language
- typography
- graphics
- titles
- color
- texture
- grading
- motion

Direction

Taste
- what feels cheap
- what feels overly produced
- what feels derivative
- what feels culturally authentic
- what feels emotionally true
- what feels cinematic
- what makes something memorable

The Taste Protocol should contain both positive and negative examples, references, case studies, annotated footage, principles, heuristics, and creative reasoning.

# III. HUMAN → AI CREATIVE INTELLIGENCE

The Taste Protocol ultimately becomes part of Vanta's technological infrastructure.

Destin does not need to build the underlying AI systems.

The CEO/technical organization owns the engineering.

Destin owns the creative intelligence those systems are attempting to reproduce.

This creates a continuous collaboration:

Destin defines taste → Engineering encodes taste → AI produces media → Destin evaluates output → Protocol improves → System improves.

Destin therefore serves as the primary human evaluator of Vanta's automated creative systems.

When the system produces something mediocre, the question becomes:

> What creative knowledge was missing that allowed the system to make this decision?

That missing knowledge should then become another improvement to the Taste Protocol.

The protocol therefore becomes a living creative system rather than a static creative handbook.

---

# IV. CREATIVE QUALITY CONTROL

Destin serves as Vanta's ultimate creative quality authority.

He establishes and maintains the standard for what deserves to carry the Vanta name.

This includes reviewing:

- Vanta Originals
- commissioned programming
- creator productions
- emerging-director productions
- AI-generated edits
- trailers
- promotional media
- title sequences
- major visual campaigns

Not every production needs to look identical.

In fact, they should not.

The objective is consistent quality without aesthetic homogeneity.

A documentary, comedy series, fishing program, fashion film, cultural documentary, and experimental film may look completely different while still satisfying the Vanta standard.

# IX. PROGRAMMING PORTFOLIO STRATEGY

Destin should manage Vanta's content as a portfolio rather than a collection of isolated productions.

A healthy programming slate may intentionally contain a mixture of:

Audience Acquisition Programming  
Creators with existing audiences who bring viewers onto Vanta.

Prestige Programming  
Exceptional work that establishes Vanta's creative reputation.

Original IP  
Formats and series created internally that Vanta can own and expand.

Emerging Talent Programming  
Low-cost asymmetric bets on creators Vanta believes can become culturally important.

Experimental Programming  
Projects used to discover entirely new media formats.

This allows Vanta to simultaneously optimize for:

Audience + Culture + Quality + IP + Experimentation.

# X. CONTENT PIPELINE

Destin owns a continuously developing pipeline of potential programming.

At any given time, Vanta should know:

Who are we watching?  
Potential creators and directors.

Who are we talking to?  
Active recruitment.

What are we developing?  
Concepts currently being shaped.

What are we producing?  
Active productions.

What is releasing?  
Current programming slate.

What did we learn?  
Creative and audience insights from released work.

The goal is to prevent Vanta's programming strategy from becoming reactive.

There should always be another generation of programming being discovered.

# XIII. LONG-TERM EVOLUTION OF THE ROLE

The role should expand with Vanta.

### Phase 1 — Build the Programming

Personally recruit creators, develop concepts, make productions, identify emerging directors, and establish the first Vanta programming slate.

### Phase 2 — Codify the Taste

Build the Vanta Taste Protocol and work closely with engineering to translate creative judgment into machine-readable creative intelligence.

### Phase 3 — Build the Studio

Recruit producers, directors, editors, cinematographers, creative operators, and other personnel as production volume grows.

### Phase 4 — Build the Creative Institution

Operate Vanta Studios as a scalable creative organization capable of discovering talent, producing original IP, partnering with established creators, and continuously generating culturally important media.

At maturity, Destin should no longer need to personally touch every production.

Instead, he should have built a creative institution that reproduces his standard at scale.

# The Core Division of Responsibility

The simplest definition of the partnership is:

CEO — builds the machine.  
Owns the technology, product, infrastructure, economics, and company architecture.

Destin — determines what the machine creates.  
Owns programming, creators, directors, taste, culture, and the studio.

Together, those functions form Vanta:

> Technology capable of producing and distributing media at unprecedented scale, governed by a creative system capable of ensuring that scale does not destroy taste.
